LAND 101 · 5 MIN READ

Land titles in Nigeria,
explained simply.

The essential words and documents to understand before you make a commitment.

BY HAY PROPERTIES·JULY 2026·5 MIN READ

A property title is the document trail that supports a seller’s right to transfer land. The most useful place to start is with what can be verified, not what sounds impressive.

Start with the document trail.

Before payment, request the documents, check the survey information and arrange a site visit. A reliable property advisor will make this process clear and never rush you through it.

“The best property decision is one you can explain clearly to yourself.”HAY PROPERTIES

Ask questions that protect your future.

Ask what document supports the seller’s ownership, whether the land is free from known disputes and how the surrounding location is likely to change. Good answers should be specific, supported by records and easy to understand.
