How do I know the land is genuine?

Every Hay property is subject to due diligence. We share title documents and explain verification clearly before you commit.

Can I pay in instalments?

Many land offers include flexible payment plans. Your advisor will talk you through the available options and milestones.

What property types do you offer?

Our collection includes land, agro real estate, residential homes and commercial property opportunities.

When do I get allocated my plot?

Allocation depends on the estate, your payment plan and the documentation stage. We keep every milestone clear from the beginning.

Can I inspect a property before I buy?

Yes. We encourage site inspections and arrange them with an advisor so you can see the location and ask the questions that matter.
